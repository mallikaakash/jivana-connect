## PITCH — Same for all intros (~45–55 sec)

**Say after your chosen intro:**

So — think about what happens **after** a patient leaves a clinic.

The consultation might be over, but the treatment journey often isn’t — that’s **continued care**. They might need another appointment, time to recover after a procedure, or a plan that runs across several visits.

But once they leave the clinic, staying connected often depends on the **patient**. If they miss the next appointment, postpone treatment, or have a concern they never report, the clinic may not know what’s happening.

And in a country like India, the pressure on each clinician is real. Even with more doctors graduating every year, **allopathic availability** is still often on the order of **roughly one doctor for every twelve hundred people** — and those doctors are not evenly spread. Attending to everyone who needs **ongoing** follow-up, not just the visit in front of you, is genuinely hard.

On top of that, **personal, empathetic follow-up** — the kind patients remember — takes time and people. For most clinics, that level of care connect is **overhead they simply can’t afford** at scale: extra staff, extra hours, extra phone calls, with no single place to track it all.

So there’s a gap: you treat someone when they’re in the clinic, but you’re not always connected with them through the rest of the journey — and **continued care** slips.

**That’s the problem we’re trying to solve with Jivana Connect.**

Jivana Connect is a voice-first AI patient engagement platform for clinics and healthcare providers. Our AI voice assistant, **Jiya**, helps automate patient calls, coordinate appointments, run follow-ups, and capture concerns that need the care team’s attention.

Instead of communication only when a patient calls back or walks in again, clinics can be **more proactive** — and much of that **remotely**. Your team doesn’t need the patient in the chair to confirm tomorrow’s slot, check recovery after a procedure, or log a reschedule. Jiya places the call; the outcome lands in the dashboard — front desk and doctors can act from the clinic or from wherever they’re working. The patient stays home; the clinic stays in the loop.

We also care how it **feels** on the line. Jiya is built to sound **personal and humane** — like a careful clinic call, not a blunt robocall — while still following the task and capturing what was said.

The goal: communication that’s easier to run for the clinic, better visibility when someone may need attention, and a more connected experience for patients — without every touchpoint meaning another visit.

Alright — **dental** example, one patient, **Ajay**. I’ll register him and we’ll place a live call.

**Stat note (if asked):** ~1:1,263 allopathic availability — Economic Survey 2024-25 / NMC registered doctors (~13.86L, ~80% availability). Government figures that include AYUSH are more favorable (~1:811); use the line above if you want the **capacity pressure** angle without a debate on definitions.

## CALL 1 — Confirmation (~50 sec)

**On screen:** **Tasks & Follow-ups** → open **Confirm appointment — …** for Ajay → show **Pending** → **Start Jiya call** → confirm.

**Say (brief):**

Jiya will call on behalf of the dental team — live outbound. Listen for the tone: we want it to feel **personal**, like someone from the clinic checking in, not a generic IVR.

**On the phone as Ajay — keep it normal:**

- Yes, this is Ajay.
- When she asks about **tomorrow at 4** (or whatever you booked): *I can’t make it — I need to **reschedule**.*

Let her finish. Don’t rush.

**Say after hang-up:**

That’s the missed-slot problem in miniature — Ajay didn’t have to come in or wait on hold. Jiya reached him **remotely**, and the clinic has the reschedule request **on record**, not on a sticky note.

## CALL 2 — After treatment (~35 sec)

**On screen:** Open the **post-procedure check-in** task (create one first if you skipped the montage):

- Type: **Post-procedure check-in**
- Title: e.g. **After root canal visit — how’s the tooth?**
- Context: follow-up after yesterday’s visit; ask about pain, chewing, swelling; no clinical advice on the call
- Due: today

→ **Start Jiya call**

**On the phone as Ajay:**

- When she asks how he’s doing: *It’s okay… but it’s **still a bit sore** when I chew on that side.*

**Say after:**

That’s the patient who **doesn’t call the clinic** but would answer if someone rang — again, **remotely**, from his side and from yours. We’re not diagnosing on the phone; we’re **capturing** what he said so the team can decide next steps.

If Ajay had said something that needs a dentist’s judgment — pain getting worse, wrong meds, whatever — it shouldn’t die in a transcript. **Clinical review** is where that lands. Jiya records; **your team** decides.

## CLOSING (~35 sec)

**On screen:** **Front desk** → **Today** (queue / recent activity) → optional title card.

**Say:**

That gap — and **continued care** — shows up on patients like Ajay. Multi-step treatment, visits in between, things he might never say unless someone reaches out with a **human-sounding** check-in.

Scale that across a practice, in a system where doctors are already stretched thin. Most clinics can’t hire their way to personalized follow-up for everyone.

**Jivana Connect** is for that **remote** layer — confirmations, follow-ups, empathetic check-ins — without another visit for every conversation. When something needs a clinician, it’s **on the record** and in the right queue — not lost on a personal mobile.

We’re not replacing the dentist or the in-person visit when care requires it. We’re trying to make **continued care** practical: connected, trackable, and as personal as we can make it on a phone call.
